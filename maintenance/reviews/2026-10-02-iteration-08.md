# Iteration 08: reject contradictory compiler trace evidence

Reviewed base: `58ae7cd79603a4ab32c4c08392a6ffa9a87a2976` (PR #46).
This focused maintenance branch is independent of the SVM and total-tree fixes.

The standard-library compiler auditor checked digest/bitwise flags and required
finite, nonnegative numerical differences. It did not check their consistency.
An unchanged tensor digest could carry a positive reported difference while the
summary still passed the exact-fidelity track. A compiled step identical to the
known finite reference could also be marked nonfinite without rejecting the
contradictory record. Changed logits could hide a contradiction in unchanged
`y` or `z` tensors because only the combined bitwise flag was checked.

The auditor now rejects positive differences for each finite tensor whose digest
matches the reference, and rejects a nonfinite flag for a completely identical
reference step. It does not impose the converse: different bytes can have zero
numerical difference, including signed zero. Consistent genuinely nonfinite
records remain accepted as failed outcomes, preserving unfavorable evidence.
No compiler, model, timing protocol, numerical tolerance or default backend changes.

Twenty dependency-light synthetic cases cover all four arms and three tensor
fields, partial-step contradictions, signed-zero and nonzero finite differences,
and a consistent nonfinite failure. Against the prior implementation, **16 failed
their rejection expectations and 4 controls passed**. All pass after the fix.
The fixture uses fabricated timing/accounting records, not new measurements.
