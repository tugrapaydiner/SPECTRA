# Iteration 09: validate trained-FP receipt identities and exhausted budgets

Reviewed base: `fb3750bd9bd7eea4fb5068a240c832cb74bfe871` in PR #49.

The trained-FP auditor accepted Boolean/floating schedule indices through Python
numeric equality, did not validate model seed/block types, and allowed different
original inputs to share one problem identity in its paired bootstrap. It also
accepted failed neural solves labeled `budget_exhausted` after only one, two or
three steps, despite this fixed experiment's four-step budget.

The auditor now validates integer schedule indices and seeds, positive integer
block counts, consistent original inputs within each family/problem identity,
and completion of all four steps before a failed neural attempt can claim budget
exhaustion. Valid early success remains allowed. Complete failed attempts remain
in the analysis and charged-cost accounting; they are not discarded or retried.
The symbolic arm's zero-step contract is unchanged.

Sixteen synthetic cases initially produced **14 failures and 2 passes**: malformed
receipts were accepted, while the valid clustered matrix and complete failed
attempts were correctly retained. All cases pass with the fix. They are fabricated
accounting fixtures, not measurements or new task-performance observations.
