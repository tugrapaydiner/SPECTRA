# Flagship gate ledger — frozen WAP support-query result

Status vocabulary:

- **PASS:** directly established by frozen evidence.
- **PASS, NARROW:** established only for the exact published workload and claim.
- **PARTIAL:** meaningful evidence exists, but the original broad gate is not complete.
- **OPEN:** not yet established.
- **NOT APPLICABLE:** the retained flagship configuration does not make that claim.

This ledger applies to the WAP support-query result. It does not rehabilitate unrelated neural, low-bit, custom-kernel, SVM, general-CNF, or MCTS tracks.

## Decision

### Result-level decision

**PASS: legitimate flagship research result, within the exact published contract.**

The result has a central contribution, prospectively unopened application instances, a strong persistent comparator, a large full-cost effect, exact whole-graph inference, complete witnesses, independent relation verification, durable evidence, and visible negative boundaries.

### System-level decision

**NOT ESTABLISHED: SPECTRA as a generally superior reasoning system.**

The result does not rescue unrelated tracks or justify the repository’s broad historical branding. A release should lead with this one result and either demote or clearly separate unsupported “general reasoner,” low-bit, MCTS, general SAT, and universal CPU-superiority claims.

### Remaining highest-value work

1. Independent outside-team replication.
2. Authentic optical availability/restriction traces or a partner-defined service workload.
3. Ordinary-hardware and non-x86 reproduction.
4. Stronger prior-art review around original-address repeated-query compilation.
5. A narrow release/README that cannot be confused with broad system superiority.
